Good to know

Do I need to know what to wear?

Not at all. Our stylists select looks from our private archive based on your mood, body and location — you simply try them on.

Can I choose the location?

Yes. Pick from Downtown, Desert, Beach, Old Dubai or Night Dubai — or tell us your idea and we will plan it.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 3–5 days ahead so we can prepare your looks and fitting. Last-minute requests are possible on availability.

Is a fitting included?

Yes. Every shoot includes a fitting to try your selected looks and adjust the details.

Do you offer hair & makeup?

Beauty touch-ups — hair and makeup — are available upon request.

Can I get videos too?

Yes. Reels and short-form video are available as an add-on.

Do you work with couples or groups?

Absolutely. Message us with the number of people and we will tailor the experience.

What is the price?

Every shoot is tailored. Message us on WhatsApp with your date and ideas and we will send you a personal quote.
